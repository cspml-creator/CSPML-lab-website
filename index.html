Who We Are

About the Group

We are a research group at the Department of Electrical, Electronics & Communication Engineering (EECE), IIT Dharwad, working at the intersection of wireless communications, statistical signal processing, and machine learning.

Our research spans next-generation wireless communications (5G/6G, massive MIMO, semantic communications), machine learning and AI, speech and audio processing, image processing and computer vision, control systems, cyber-physical security, and intelligent autonomous systems. We partner with national funding agencies, defence research organisations, and global academic institutions to translate theory into real-world impact.

What We Do

Research Areas

Our work spans theoretical foundations to real-world systems, tackling open problems at the intersection of communications, signal processing, and machine learning.

🤖

Machine Learning & AI

Advancing large language models, generative AI, and deep learning for intelligent inference — with a focus on federated and distributed learning, bilevel optimization, and foundation model adaptation for communication and sensing applications.

📡

Wireless Communications & Networks

Designing next-generation 5G/6G systems spanning massive MIMO, NOMA, semantic and beyond-Shannon communications, energy harvesting, age-of-information metrics, and blockchain-based resource management.

🔊

Signal & Speech Processing

Advancing statistical signal processing for speech enhancement, speaker recognition, handwriting analysis, and audio processing, with applications in human-computer interaction and accessibility.

🖼️

Image Processing & Computer Vision

Building intelligent visual systems for digital image analysis, SAR and remote sensing imagery, object recognition, and pattern classification for earth observation and defence applications.

🎛️

Control Systems & Cyber-Physical Security

Developing robust decentralized and optimal control strategies, securing networked cyber-physical systems against adversarial attacks, and co-designing communication and control with post-quantum security guarantees.

🚗

Intelligent Transportation & Autonomous Systems

Enabling safe autonomous navigation for UAVs and mobile robots in GPS-denied environments, and designing intelligent connected vehicle networks with delay-aware control for future transportation infrastructure.
