Accepting Applications Internship Jul 2025

💡 Research Internships

Step into real research — gain hands-on experience in AI, communications, and signal processing alongside our expert team, working with state-of-the-art equipment in a vibrant and inspiring lab environment.

  • Duration: 4 – 12 weeks (flexible).
  • Areas: Machine learning, 5G/6G communications, signal processing, computer vision, control systems.
  • Eligibility: Undergraduate or postgraduate students with a solid academic background.
  • Mode: On-site at IIT Dharwad campus.

To apply, send your CV and a brief statement of interest to cspml@iitdh.ac.in with the subject line "Research Internship Application".
